A FIELD GUIDE

A little light goes a long way.

Lead Aldric, Lyra, and Sera through three generated floors. Defeat the Ash Warden on the final floor to reclaim the Emberheart.

01 · Explore

Use WASD / arrow keys or click a revealed floor tile. Your party stops at encounters and discoveries. Step onto a shrine or stairwell, then press Space or the action button.

02 · Fight

Each living hero takes one action per round, in any order. Select a hero (1–3), choose an ability, and select its target. Enemies act after your last hero. Their intentions show their next attack.

03 · Work together

Bulwark halves all enemy damage this round. Hunter’s Mark adds 3 damage to the next two hits. Ember Wave hits all enemies. Specials need one full round to recharge.

04 · Survive

Draughts heal 18 HP and cost an action in battle. Shrines heal and revive everyone by 18 HP. Victories heal living heroes by 4 HP. Descending grants temporary resolve (+5 max HP, +1 attack) and restores 12 HP, including fallen heroes. Return to town to sell treasure, equip gear, recruit or swap heroes, and spend XP on permanent training. Difficulty is chosen on the expedition board.

05 · Choose your weapons

In town, open Weapons to buy or swap gear and choose your grip. Spears favor Pierce, axes favor Cut, maces and hammers favor Blunt, and swords balance all three. Small melee weapons use one hand, large use two, and medium allow either (+2 attack in two hands). Rangers use crossbows, shortbows, or longbows. Arcanists use one-handed wands or two-handed staffs.

No timers. No rush. Progress saves automatically in this browser when local storage is available. Your campaign persists between expeditions. New campaign resets it.

 A FRESH BEGINNING

Start a new campaign?

This resets your fellowship, levels, equipment, gold, and dungeon progress. To keep your progress, return to town and use the expedition board instead.

 THE ROAD HOME

Return to Hearthglen?

You will leave this dungeon behind. Your recovered treasure, gold, XP, equipment, and recruits are kept. Everyone will rest and revive at the inn. Your next departure begins a new dungeon.
