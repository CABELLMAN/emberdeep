A FIELD GUIDE

A little light goes a long way.

Lead Aldric, Lyra, and Sera through three generated floors. Defeat the Ash Warden on the final floor to reclaim the Emberheart.

01 · Explore

Use WASD / arrow keys or click a revealed floor tile. Your party stops at encounters and discoveries. Step onto a shrine or stairwell, then press Space or the action button.

02 · Fight

Each living hero takes one action per round, in any order. Select a hero (1–3), choose an ability, and select its target. Enemies act after your last hero. Their intentions show their next attack.

03 · Work together

Bulwark halves all enemy damage this round. Hunter’s Mark adds 3 damage to the next two hits. Ember Wave hits all enemies. Specials need one full round to recharge.

04 · Survive

Draughts heal 18 HP and cost an action in battle. Shrines heal and revive everyone by 18 HP. Victories heal living heroes by 4 HP. Descending raises your level and restores 12 HP, including fallen heroes.

No timers. No rush. Progress saves automatically in this browser when local storage is available. New expeditions replace the current save.
